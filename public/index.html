👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Zone

🛡️ Safety first, always

  • No public chat and no messaging between children — ever.
  • No public profiles, no leaderboards, no ads, and no external links for children.
  • Accounts use only a child-chosen username and a PIN. No child email, no real name, no personal data.
  • PINs are stored only as strong one-way hashes (scrypt) on the server — never in plain text.
  • Progress is personal: stars, badges and the treehouse celebrate your child's own journey.

🔒 Privacy

WonderNest collects the minimum needed to save progress: a username, a hashed PIN, an age group, a chosen explorer character, and activity progress. Nothing is sold or shared. A grown-up math check guards account creation and this Parent Zone.

♿ Accessibility

Use the controls at the top of every page for bigger text, high contrast, and calm mode (reduced motion). Buttons are large for small hands and touch screens, and the layout works on tablets, phones, and computers.
